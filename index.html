NumIQ Privacy Policy Effective date: 4 October 2026. Publisher: Sunit Kumar Giria. Applies to NumIQ 4.1, package com.sunitkumargiria.numiq. Contact: casunitgiria@gmail.com. Local game data NumIQ gameplay works offline without an account. Progress, answers, scores, unfinished rounds, favourites and preferences remain in private device storage. Game answers and round records are not sent to the publisher or Google. Advertising and data sharing This version uses Google AdMob banner ads on menu screens for users who select 18 or older. Ads require internet. Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP address (used to estimate general location), app and ad interactions, performance and diagnostic information, advertising ID, app set ID and applicable device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Transfers use TLS encryption. Google processes this information under its own policies. See Google Privacy Policy. NumIQ does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or shared files. Age group and consent NumIQ is intended for ages 13 and older. Users who select Under 18 play without ads; the app does not initialize the advertising SDK for them. The selected age group is stored locally; no birth date is requested. For adults, Google User Messaging Platform checks whether a consent form is required before ads can load. Where required, Settings → Ad privacy choices lets you revisit your decision. Settings → Ads age group lets you change your group. Ad consent preferences may be stored by Google and on the device. Advertising identifiers can also be reset or deleted through Android settings. Security, retention and deletion Android backups are disabled. Game data remains until cleared or the app is uninstalled. Settings → Delete all saved data removes game progress and preferences; it does not delete Google's advertising records or consent preferences. Clear NumIQ's app storage or uninstall to remove local game, age-group and consent preferences. For Google's records and retention practices, consult Google's privacy policy and privacy controls. There are no NumIQ accounts. Support Contact Sunit Kumar Giria at casunitgiria@gmail.com for privacy questions or support. If you email us, we use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry. Google Play and Android may independently process information under their own policies. Changes We will update this policy and store disclosures when data practices change.
